What drives us

Founded on the principle of technical excellence, Chua Micro Tech began as a specialized laboratory for the hardware challenges standard repair shops couldn't handle, and now writes the software its own bench runs on.

Mission

To provide the highest standard of micro-engineering, component-level repair and purpose-built software, extending the lifecycle of premium technology through precision science.

Vision

To become Malaysia's leading independent authority in drones hardware repair, high-performance computer maintenance and the software that supports them.

Our story

Redefining component-level precision in Kulim.

We don't just swap parts; we diagnose at the circuit level. From rescuing liquid-damaged motherboards to recalibrating high-performance drone flight controllers, our bench is equipped with industry-leading diagnostic tools to ensure every device returns to factory specifications. Where the right tool did not exist, we wrote it: CMT Drone Service Utility started on this bench.
