    Ask us on WhatsApp
    What is CMT Drone Service Utility?

    A Windows desktop app for drone technicians. It reads a connected aircraft, shows live telemetry, edits parameters, tests every module, analyses flight logs and runs guided calibration and firmware procedures.

    Will every tool work on my aircraft?

    Not always. Tools depend on the model and its firmware. The firmware flasher keeps profiles for the aircraft listed above. Send us your model and firmware version and we will tell you what applies.

    Can software fix physical damage?

    No. A broken gimbal motor, cable or board needs a hardware repair first. The app helps you find the fault and finish the job once the hardware is sound.

    How do the plans differ?

    Only in duration: Monthly runs 30 days, Yearly 365 days, and Permanent does not expire. Each opens the full workspace on one workstation.

    Does it need an internet connection?

    Yes, for sign-in, licence checks, updates and calibration file analysis, which runs on the CMT server so your original files stay untouched.

    Which languages does it support?

    English, Bahasa Melayu, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish and Tamil.

    Is it made or approved by DJI?

    No. It is developed independently by Chua Micro Tech and is not affiliated with, authorised or endorsed by DJI or any other manufacturer.
